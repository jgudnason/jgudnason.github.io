This site replaces the earlier single-page version of `jongudnason.com`. It is built as a static
site, and all content lives as Markdown in the repository, which keeps updates fast and the
publication history version-controlled.

## What is here

- **Research** — themes, lab affiliations, and collaborations.
- **Teaching** — current courses, graduate supervision, and project suggestions for students.
- **Activities** — dispatches from research visits and conferences, plus announcements.
- **Publications** — papers grouped by year with DOI links.

Prospective students should start with the project suggestions on the
[Teaching](/teaching/) page.
