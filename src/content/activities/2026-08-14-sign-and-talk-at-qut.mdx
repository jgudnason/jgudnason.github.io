## The visit

On 14 August 2026 I presented the Sign and Talk project at Queensland University of Technology,
to Dr Jessica Korte and her collaborators Simon Denman and Sean Maher. The talk was well
received, and I plan to follow up with them soon.

## Student exchange

Separately, on 17 August, I met with Truc Huynh, QUT's International Student Mobility
Coordinator, and presented exchange opportunities to eighteen prospective students.
