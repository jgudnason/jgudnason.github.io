## The visit

Between 27 July and 14 August 2026 I visited Professor Janet Wiles at the University of
Queensland, dropping by her lab a couple of times a week.

## Conversations

Janet and I met several times to compare notes on our respective research, including her lab's
work on mycelial networks and language technology for underresourced languages. I also met with
Dr Chelsea Dobbins, who works on behaviour and emotion monitoring, and made the case for speech
as an additional signal in that work.
